Frequently Asked Questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes! Hope & Impact is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. All donations are 100% tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. You'll receive an email receipt immediately after your donation.

How is my donation used?

90% of every donation goes directly to our programs, with 10% covering essential administrative and fundraising costs. You can choose specific programs or allow us to allocate funds where they're needed most.

Can I cancel my monthly donation?

Yes, you can cancel your monthly donation at any time by contacting us or logging into your donor account. There are no penalties or fees for cancellation.

Will I receive updates about my impact?

Absolutely! We'll send you quarterly impact reports with stories, photos, and statistics showing how your donation is making a difference. You can opt out of these updates at any time.
