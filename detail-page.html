Maria's Story

Maria is a bright and cheerful 8-year-old girl living in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. She lives with her grandmother in a small house on the outskirts of the city. Despite facing difficult circumstances, Maria maintains a positive outlook and dreams of a better future.

Her mother passed away when she was just 4 years old, and her father works in a neighboring province, only able to visit once every few months. Maria's grandmother, who is 68 years old, does her best to provide for Maria by selling vegetables at the local market, but the income is often not enough to cover basic necessities.

At school, Maria excels in mathematics and has a natural curiosity for learning. Her teachers describe her as one of the most dedicated students, always eager to help her classmates. She dreams of becoming a teacher one day so she can help other children in her community.

In her free time, Maria loves to draw and read whatever books she can find. She's particularly fascinated by stories about far-away places and different cultures. Your sponsorship can help Maria continue her education and work toward achieving her dreams.

Her Community

Maria lives in a low-income neighborhood where many families struggle with poverty. Limited access to clean water, healthcare, and educational resources are common challenges. Most children in her area work to support their families instead of attending school.

How You Help

Your monthly sponsorship of $32 provides Maria with access to quality education, nutritious meals, healthcare, and personal mentorship. You'll also help her family with basic necessities and create opportunities for her entire community.
